2:T48f,Time-series data describe the dynamic evolution of the real world and are widely used in transportation, energy, environment, industry, finance, and urban computing. Traditional task-specific modeling limits knowledge transfer across datasets, domains, and tasks. Time-Series Foundation Models (TSFMs), enabled by large-scale pre-training, unified representations, and zero-/few-shot adaptation, have formed a new paradigm for general-purpose temporal intelligence, expanding from forecasting to anomaly detection, multimodal analysis, question answering, and reasoning. Organized around the AGREE principles (Automation, Generalization, Robustness, Explainability, and Efficiency), the tutorial discusses generative and multimodal modeling, spatio-temporal applications, and temporal reasoning. b:T5ca,Geospatial analytics is an important field in many application domains, including epidemiology, transportation science, criminology, and urban science. Therefore, many software packages and systems, e.g., QGIS, ArcGIS, SuperMap, and spatstat, can also support these geospatial analysis tools. However, since (1) these tools suffer from high time complexities and (2) many large-scale location datasets can be collected by domain experts nowadays, these software packages and systems are inefficient (or even infeasible) to support large-scale location datasets. As such, we conduct this tutorial to arouse the interest from those spatiotemporal data management researchers and practitioners for (i) developing efficient algorithms for these tools and (ii) using our fast QGIS plugins and our preliminary system, based on our efficient algorithms, to analyze their large-scale location datasets, respectively. This tutorial consists of four parts. In the first part, we will provide an overview of geospatial analysis tools. In the second part, we will specifically discuss efficient algorithms for kernel density visualization (one of the tools) and its variants, which can possibly be extended to support other tools. In the third part, we will provide hands-on experience for testing the efficiency of existing software packages/systems and our QGIS plugins/preliminary system (by conducting some case studies).
