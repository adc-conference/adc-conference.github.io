Social Event - Chimelong Ocean Kingdom

We are thrilled to announce that the ADC 2026 Hengqin Venue Social Event will take place at the spectacular Chimelong Ocean Kingdom in Hengqin!

  • Date: Wednesday, December 16, 2026
  • Time: 16:00 - 21:00 (buses depart from the conference venue at 15:30)
  • Admission: Free admission and a buffet dinner are included for all registered in-person full conference delegates.

Discover the World’s Largest Marine Theme Park

Located in Hengqin, Chimelong Ocean Kingdom is a world-class destination where oceanic wonder, cutting-edge thrills, and marine conservation come alive.

During the event, you will get to journey through eight immersive themed zones:

  • Ocean Wonders: Home to the awe-inspiring Whale Shark Aquarium, which features the world’s largest aquarium tank, gentle whale sharks, and majestic manta rays.
  • Polar Horizon: Witness the icy majesty of meticulously recreated Arctic habitats, home to rescued polar bears, belugas, and penguins.
  • Thrills & Entertainment: Soar through rainforest canopies on the Parrot Coaster, plunge into the abyss on the Tower of Sea Monsters, and witness the magic of the Ocean Kingdom Journey of Lights Parade — where solar-powered, eco-friendly floats illuminate the night.

Entertainment with a Purpose

What truly sets Chimelong Ocean Kingdom apart is its unwavering commitment to blending entertainment with conservation. Every ride, show, and exhibit is designed to inspire environmental action. During your visit, you can:

  • Symbolically adopt a rescued sea turtle, manatee, or walrus.
  • Track real-time coral restoration via interactive tech kiosks.
  • Support “Adopt-a-Habitat” programs that fund crucial seagrass rehabilitation.
  • Experience immersive shows like Symphony of Ocean and Deep Sea Odyssey, which use advanced projection mapping and environmental data to transform storytelling into stewardship.

We look forward to an unforgettable evening of networking, fun, and discovery with you!
